Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Circle (circle-blr.netlify.app) is a free app for sharing honest restaurant ratings with your friends in Bangalore. This page explains, in plain words, what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Your name. The name you type when you join, or — if you sign in with Google — the name, email address and profile picture from your Google account.
  • What you post. Your ratings, scores, the dish you recommend, your notes and any photos you upload.
  • Your lists and friends. The places you save to Want to Try, places you add to the map, and who is in your circle.
  • Which days you open the app. When you're signed in we note the date you opened Circle, so we can count how many people use it. This is private — it is never shown to other users.
  • Anonymous visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and which pages are opened. It uses no cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and doesn't build a profile of you.
  • Your location — only if you ask. When you tap “my location” on the map, your browser shares your position with the app to centre the map. It is not stored.

What other people can see

  • Your name, ratings, notes, dishes and photos are visible to everyone using Circle (that's how Trending and Top lists work).
  • Your Want to Try list is private — only you can see it.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

Where it is stored

  • Accounts, ratings and photos are stored with Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • The website is served by Netlify.
  • Maps come from OpenStreetMap; when you search for a place we send the search text to Photon (komoot) to find matches.
  • Restaurant listings come from the open Overture Maps dataset.

These services may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect their systems.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads and don't allow paid or sponsored reviews.
  • We don't use tracking cookies. Your browser stores a small sign-in token so you stay logged in.

Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, we only ask Google for your basic profile (name, email and profile picture) to create your account. We don't access your contacts, Gmail, Drive or anything else, and we don't share Google data with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and everything you posted at any time using the support email shown on Circle's Google sign-in screen. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Circle is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
